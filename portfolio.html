03 — Bachelor’s thesis

The Strategic Importance of Product Adaptation in the Video Game Industry

How do video game companies balance globally standardized products with the different preferences, regulations and expectations of international markets?

Global products, local adjustments. Rather than developing fundamentally different games for each market, companies tend to adapt localization, monetization, marketing and selected content.
More than translation. Localization increasingly forms part of broader product strategy, influenced by consumer preferences, player engagement and ongoing development.
A constantly evolving process. Regulatory requirements, platform ecosystems and changing player behavior make international adaptation an ongoing challenge.
